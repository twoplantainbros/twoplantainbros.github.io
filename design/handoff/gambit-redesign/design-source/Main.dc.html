About Gambit

Our chips break out of boundaries.

We understand what makes plantain chips so good, that’s why we’re not afraid to take a gambit with new shapes and flavors. We’ve run tests day after day for months to narrow down on the shapes and flavors we have for Gambits today, and we’ve landed on something special. Try one and you’ll get it too.

Our gambit

It all started when Himanshu grabbed a bag of “Hot and Spicy” Plantain Chips to get a taste of childhood. But those chips sucked. The chips we grew up with were crunchy, salty and always around for us. We realized we took those chips for granted.

As we scoured the aisles and saw rows and rows of fancy new flavors and shapes on potato chip we realized that plantain chips had been left behind and we set out to change that.

Our first chips sucked. But each batch was better than the last and we didn’t stop when we reached “good”. We strove to break boundaries with these chips in the pursuit of something “great” and we landed at waffle-cut with crazy good flavors like Churro and BBQ.

Try one and you’ll get it too.
